## Fred's decisions (2026-11-03)

- Accepted every recommendation above: Lobster Nebula on NGC 6357, Butterfly
  Nebula on NGC 6302, the three `remove` rows, LBN 191's two names, and the
  single-source names left out.
- Overrode three drops:
  - **NGC 2264 is the Cone Nebula** (now its preferred name; SIMBAD's Cone
    Nebula record sits at NGC 2264's position).
  - **IC 1396 is the Elephant's Trunk Nebula** (SIMBAD gives the name to
    IC 1396A, inside IC 1396).
  - **NGC 1931 is the Fly Nebula** (Wikipedia). Rows resting on his decision
    cite `Fred:2026-11-03` as a source.
- The shipped list: 204 names for 173 objects and groups, plus 3 removals.

## Codex's independent check (records `22`–`23`, 2026-11-03)

Fred asked for a second opinion on the open names. Codex (read-only, with web
search) rated 44 of 45 established, and one wrong: **Pickering's Triangle is
not NGC 6979** but Fleming's Triangular Wisp about 35′ southwest (a historical
misidentification). It stays out. Codex's verdict is not a source; Claude
fetched each page it cited and kept only qualifying sources whose text shows
both the name and the object's designation (PDFs searched within ±400
characters). One organisation counts once: two NASA pages are one source.
Links to unauthorised copies of books were not used.
