# v0.51.0 — Object details: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** Codex implements one phase at a time under
> `docs/agent-collaboration.md` §4; Claude reviews each phase. Steps use
> checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax. Never stage, commit or push.

**Goal:** each project's targets show their catalog numbers, constellation,
distance and Wikipedia link, with a distance ruler. They appear on Gallery
card backs, in the list view, on the full-size viewer's details side
(Gallery and Messier pages) and on a new project Info tab. Distances that
mislead are dropped or corrected, and a hung Open-Meteo server costs about
21 s instead of a minute.

**Architecture:**
- **Backend facts:** one loader builds each target's facts and each
  plate-solved picture's other objects. `api/object_facts_db.py` holds the
  DB loaders and `services/object_facts.py` the pure rules. The Gallery
  response and a new `GET /api/projects/{project_id}/info` both use it.
- **Catalog data:** the redshift backfill skips z < 0.01. Curated rows add
  the Messier and Caldwell distances (supplied by Claude) and correct
  IC 3418.
- **Frontend:**
  - pure formatting and ruler-layout helpers (`lib/objectFacts.ts`,
    `lib/distanceRuler.ts`);
  - one `DistanceRuler` component drawn at five sizes;
  - shared target-row pieces, used by the card back, the list row, the
    viewer's details side and `ProjectInfoTab`.

**Tech stack:** Python 3.14, FastAPI, Pydantic, aiosqlite; React,
TypeScript, MUI, TanStack Query. No new dependency, no migration.

**Spec:** [object details design](../specs/2026-11-13-v0.51.0-object-details-design.md).
Mockups (local, ignored; the built UI must look like them):
`.superpowers/mockups/v0.51.0/card-details.html`, `deep-field.html`,
`gallery-list.html` and `project-info.html`. `project-info.html` holds the
final ruler rules: the bracket, count markers, and the lane for other
objects.

**Base:** the staged v0.51.0 cleanup on top of `104888f`.
- Codex works in the worktree `.superpowers/wt-v0.51.0-details`, created
  from `104888f` with the staged cleanup applied as uncommitted changes.

**High-stakes** (`docs/agent-collaboration.md` §5):

## Global constraints

- **Light-years** = parsecs × 3.26156. Three significant figures, using
  `toThreeSigFigs` from `frontend/src/lib/distanceFormat.ts` (export it).
  - Under 1,000,000: an integer with thousands separators (`444`,
    `2,610`).
  - From a million: `22.7 million`, `36.0 million` (keep the trailing
    zero), `245 million`.
  - From a billion: `1.25 billion`.
  - Long unit `light-years`; short unit `ly`.
- **Estimate**: `distance_method == "redshift"`. Long wording
  `about 245 million light-years (estimate)`; short `about 245 million ly`.
- **Ruler scale**: log10 of light-years from 2 to 10 (100 ly to 10 billion
  ly). Clamp outside that. Band from 100 to 100,000 ly. The Info ruler's
  first tick reads `100 ly`, not `100 light-years`: the long form ran into
  `1,000` at ordinary widths (Claude's review, 2026-11-13).
- **Exact copy**:
  - band captions: `Milky Way: about 100,000 light-years across`
    (viewer, Info) and `Milky Way: about 100,000 ly across` (cards,
    list);
  - legends: `Your targets` / `Your target`;
    `Estimate worked out from redshift`;
    `Other objects found in the plate-solved picture`;
    `Several at about the same distance`;
  - the card's grey line: `{n} other object(s) found in the plate-solved
    picture`;
  - `No distance on record.`; `No distance on record for your target(s)`;
  - Info tab:
    - section titles `Target` / `Targets`, `How far away` and
      `Also in this picture`, with the note `{n} catalogued object(s),
      nearest first`;
    - the controls `Show all {n}` and `Show fewer`;
    - the unsolved heading
      `Info appears once this project is plate solved`, with the spec's
      sentence and the button `Plate solve it`;
    - the right column's caption
      `The constellation at the centre of the picture`;
  - the W badge's aria-label `{name} on Wikipedia (opens in a new tab)`;
  - the viewer's picture-area button `Show details for {title}` or
    `Show the picture`.
- **Colours**:
  - targets: theme primary (`#c07b2b` light / `#d4993f` dark);
  - other objects: `text.secondary` at about 0.6 opacity;
  - the band: `rgba(91,122,158,0.16)` light / `rgba(125,154,184,0.22)`
    dark, with captions in `#3f5c78` / `#a0b8d0`;
  - the viewer's details panel always uses the dark palette (paper
    `#24272c`, text `#e2e0dd`, secondary `#a3a09b`).

  Six-digit hex only.
- **Wikipedia links**: English (`dso_external_ref`, provider `wikipedia`,
  language `en`), with `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`. A click
  never turns a card or the viewer.
- **No new dependencies.** D3 is not needed for the ruler; it is
  absolutely positioned HTML/MUI, as in the mockups.

## Review focus

1. **Targets but no plate solve:** cards and the list show the targets'
   facts with no grey dots. The Info tab shows the unsolved state. The info
   endpoint returns `solved: false` with targets and no field. Tests: A2
   `test_info_unsolved_project`.
2. **Mixed distances:** one target with a distance and one without. The
   ruler draws one dot, the Info row says `No distance on record`, and the
   card omits nothing else. Test: A1
   `test_target_without_distance_has_none`; rendered check C2.
3. **Out-of-scale distances** (under 100 ly, over 10 billion ly) clamp to
   the ruler's ends without overflowing the card. Test: B1 helper cases;
   rendered check C2.
4. **The viewer turned over** while the picture is loading or has failed:
   it still turns. Stepping, a swipe or play shows the next picture's
   picture side. Rendered check C2.
5. **A plate solve with hundreds of objects:** the Gallery payload carries
   only distances for other objects, and the Info table pages at ten.
   Test: A1 `test_gallery_other_objects_carry_distances_only`; performance
   check C2.

  only the solve's objects with a distance, minus the targets.
  `ProjectRecord` gains the same list.
- In `api/object_facts_db.py`:
  - `async def load_target_facts(conn, project_ids: list[int] | None = None) -> dict[int, list[TargetFacts]]`:
    the gallery's current target query, extended with `distance_pc`,
    `distance_method` and the English Wikipedia URL (lowest `id` wins, as
    in `api/planner.py`). Moves the body that builds `TargetFacts` out of
    `load_project_records`. `None` means all projects.
  - `async def load_other_objects(conn, project_ids: list[int]) -> dict[int, list[OtherObject]]`:
    joins `project_solve` → `project_dso` → `dso`, excludes the project's
    `project_target` DSOs, and sorts nearest first with no-distance
    objects last (then by designation). It is for single projects (A2).
  - `async def load_other_distances(conn, project_ids: list[int] | None = None) -> dict[int, list[OtherObjectDistance]]`:
    the same join, selecting only `dso_id`, `distance_pc` and
    `distance_method`, with `distance_pc IS NOT NULL`. It is for the
    Gallery, and builds no names or URLs.
- `load_project_records(conn, *, project_ids: list[int] | None = None, with_object_facts: bool = False)`.
  - With `with_object_facts=True` (the Gallery and A2), targets carry the
    new facts and records carry `other_distances`.
  - Without it (the Messier checklist, suggestions, image search), it does
    exactly today's work.
  - Every query in it honours `project_ids`.

### Task B1: Formatting, ruler layout and the `DistanceRuler`

**Files:**

  `frontend/src/api/gallery.ts` (types for A1's fields),
  create `frontend/src/api/projectInfo.ts` (`ProjectInfo` types and
  `fetchProjectInfo(projectId: number): Promise<ProjectInfo>`)

**Interfaces:**
- `objectFacts.ts`:
  - `lightYears(pc: number): number`;
  - `formatLy(ly: number): string`, the Global constraints' wording with
    no unit;
  - `formatDistance(pc: number, estimated: boolean, form: "long" | "short", withEstimateWord: boolean): string`;
  - `typeName(objType: string | null): string`, which is `displayDsoType`
    after `filterTypeCode`;
  - `typeLine(targets: GalleryTarget[]): string`, the spec §1 rules;
  - `targetLabel(t: GalleryTarget): string` (its name);
  - `countSummary(targets: GalleryTarget[]): { head: string; names: string }`
    for more than four targets: `21 galaxies` or `21 targets`, and
    `NGC 4452, NGC 4497, IC 3470 and 18 more`.
- `distanceRuler.ts`:
  - `rulerX(ly: number): number`, a percentage from 0 to 100, clamped;
  - `groupMarkers(items: { key: string; ly: number; estimated: boolean }[], widthPx: number, markerPx: number, mergedMarkerPx: number): MarkerGroup[]`,
    where `MarkerGroup = { x: number; keys: string[]; estimated: boolean }`.
    It is the `groups()` rule in `project-info.html`, with the threshold
    taken from the rendered width:
    `thresholdPct = (markerPx + 2) / widthPx * 100`. Sort by x; a dot
    joins the current group when `x - group.x0 < thresholdPct`; the group
    x is the midpoint of its first and last members; `estimated` holds
    only for a single estimated member.
  - A second, collision pass uses the final centres and the drawn diameters
    (merged markers are larger). It merges any two neighbouring groups
    whose centres are less than `(dA + dB) / 2 + 2` px apart, and repeats
    until none overlap.
  - `DistanceRuler` measures its own width with a `ResizeObserver` and
    regroups on resize.
  - Marker diameters (px): small 8/6, card 10/7, list 10/7, viewer 13/8,
    info 13/8 (target/other); a merged marker is 19 px on the info and
    viewer sizes, 15 px on the others.
  - **Other objects with an estimated distance** are drawn as hollow grey
    rings when single, matching hollow amber targets; merged markers are
    filled. The `Estimate worked out from redshift` legend line shows
    whenever any hollow ring is drawn.
  - `valueLabel(targets: { ly: number; estimated: boolean }[], form: "long" | "short", withEstimateWord: boolean): string | null`,
    which gives a single value, or a shared-unit range `36.0–38.9 million`,
    or a mixed range `7.54 million – 1.25 billion`, followed by the unit;
  - `labelAnchor(xLo: number, xHi: number, size: RulerSize): "start" | "center" | "end"`,
    which follows the mockups: `end` when the midpoint is above 66 (70 on
    `info`), `start` below 25 (12 on `info`), otherwise `center`. The
    value label never wraps; at `start` and `end` it is pinned to the
    ruler's edge so it never overflows.
- `DistanceRuler` props:
  - `size: "small" | "card" | "list" | "viewer" | "info"`;
  - `targets: GalleryTarget[]`;
  - `others?: { dso_id: number; distance_pc: number; distance_estimated: boolean }[]`;
  - `highlightKey?: string | null`, where keys are `t:<dso_id>` and
    `o:<dso_id>`.

  It renders the scale, band, captions, tick labels, markers, value label
  and legend per the size, as in the spec §2 and the mockups' pixel
  values. Markers carry `data-keys` for highlighting.
  `role="img"` with an `aria-label` listing each target's distance.
- `TargetRows` props: `targets`, `size: "card" | "list" | "viewer"`, and
  `showBadges: boolean`. It renders the name, the catalog numbers and the
  W badge per row, or `countSummary` for more than four targets.
- `WikipediaBadge` props: `url: string` and `name: string`. It stops
  click propagation.
